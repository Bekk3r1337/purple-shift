PURPLE SHIFT VISUAL BIBLE

Create artwork for a serious modern cinematic visual novel set around a contemporary warehouse and the anomalous Purple Storm.

Core style:
- polished 2D semi-realistic anime / cinematic visual-novel illustration
- clearly illustrated artwork, never live-action photography, never photorealistic portraiture, never 3D render
- grounded modern Russian / Eastern European urban-industrial reality
- believable warehouse architecture, practical work clothing and real materials
- expressive but natural faces; avoid generic glossy AI-anime look
- coherent anatomy, hands and perspective
- cinematic light, restrained depth of field, controlled contrast
- purple is an anomaly accent, not a permanent neon wash over everything
- ordinary reality should remain readable and believable so the Storm feels intrusive

Character continuity is mandatory:
- the same named character must keep the same face geometry across every sprite and CG
- preserve eye shape, nose, jaw, hairstyle, hairline, apparent age, body proportions and uniform design
- never turn one expression variant into a different-looking person
- never age a character up or down
- never change ethnicity, hairstyle, facial structure or wardrobe unless a task explicitly requests it
- when multiple references of one character are supplied, REFERENCE 1 is the canonical identity anchor
- additional references are pose/expression/context references only
- improve rendering without redesigning the person
- all cast members must share one coherent 2D visual-novel rendering language

Purple Storm language:
- subtle impossible violet light, spatial seams, refraction, memory-like afterimages and wrong reflections
- occasional "impossible extra element" motifs, but never random fantasy magic clutter
- no medieval fantasy, no magical armor, no cyberpunk city redesign, no sci-fi spacesuits
- V-13 visuals may feel archival, surveillance-like and slightly wrong, but remain grounded in the warehouse world

Environment continuity:
- preserve location layout and major landmarks when a background reference is supplied
- do not change camera geography unless explicitly requested
- do not add text, subtitles, logos, signatures or watermarks unless explicitly requested
- no decorative borders

Production target:
The result should look like one consistent commercial visual novel made by one art team, not a collection of unrelated AI generations.
